## Document Structure

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 is organized as follows:

### Major Chapters

1. **Companies Income Tax** - Corporate taxation rules
2. **Personal Income Tax** - Individual tax obligations
3. **Value Added Tax (VAT)** - Consumption tax provisions
4. **Capital Gains Tax** - Tax on asset sales
5. **Petroleum Profits Tax** - Oil & gas sector taxation
6. **Withholding Tax** - Tax deduction at source
7. **Education Tax** - Levy for education funding
8. **Stamp Duties** - Tax on legal documents
9. **Tax Administration** - FIRS powers and procedures
10. **Penalties & Offences** - Compliance enforcement
